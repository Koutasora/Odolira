Odolira Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Odolira is an Android app for managing bikes, planning routes, and cycling navigation. It's designed to work locally: it doesn't require an account, doesn't sign in to any Odolira service (none exists), and doesn't include any analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries.

What data is collected and where it's stored

Your bikes, routes (planned, recorded, imported), bike photos, and app settings are stored only locally on your device (a local database and the app's own storage). None of this data is ever sent to an Odolira server — we don't operate one. You can delete all app data at any time from Settings ("Delete all data") or by uninstalling the app.

GPS location

The app requests location access to:

  • show your position on the map and provide turn-by-turn navigation,
  • record your ride (distance, elevation, GPS track) while recording is active,
  • calculate cycling routes based on a start and destination point.

Location is only used while the relevant feature (recording/navigation) is actively running, via a foreground service with a persistent, visible notification — the app never requests background location access and doesn't collect your position when it isn't actively in use. Coordinates needed to calculate a route or search for a place are sent to the independent third-party routing and geocoding services described below — never to an Odolira server.

Third-party services the app uses

To provide maps, place search, and route calculation, Odolira relies on independent, public third-party services. Using these features sends them the necessary data (e.g. coordinates, search text), governed by their own privacy policies, independent of Odolira:

  • OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles — map tiles,
  • BRouter — cycling route calculation and surface data,
  • Nominatim and Photon (OpenStreetMap) — address/place search and geocoding,
  • Waymarked Trails — optional cycling-route overlay (only when you enable it manually).

All geographic data originates from OpenStreetMap and services built on it.

Microphone

The app may request microphone access only when you tap the voice-search icon while entering a destination. The recording is processed by Android's built-in speech recognition service and is never saved or transmitted by Odolira.

Notifications

While recording a ride or navigating, the app shows a local notification (pause/resume/finish). These notifications are generated entirely on-device and don't come from any push server.

Data sharing

Odolira doesn't sell or share your personal data or route data with any advertising or analytics companies. The app doesn't include any third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the scope of data collected changes, this page will be updated along with a new date at the top.
